Ebru

Paper marbling on a virtual bath. Float drops of ink, pull combs and styluses through them, swirl them, and save the print. Every stroke moves the ink exactly the way an incompressible film would, so no colour ever gains or loses area.

Keyboard: with the tray focused, arrow keys move the cursor (Shift for bigger steps), Enter uses the current tool there, 1–5 pick a tool, [ and ] change the size, , and . turn the stroke direction, C picks the next ink, Z undoes and Y redoes.

Drops and tines follow Aubrey Jaffer's mathematical marbling: a drop of radius r at C moves older ink at P to C + (P − C)·√(1 + r²/|P − C|²), and a tine slides ink along its stroke by an amount that dies off with distance across it. Swirls turn each circle around a centre by an arc that fades the same way, and waves bend the bath sideways; both keep area. Each picture is drawn by running the inverse of every stroke backwards from each pixel. Source on GitHub.
